# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project community.

Be constructive, assume good intent, keep technical criticism specific, and respect the safety and privacy of other researchers and their clients. Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, credential sharing, and promotion of unauthorized access are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and interactions that violate these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's security or contact channels.
